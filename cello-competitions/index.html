What I would look at first

Not a ranking of artistic prestige only: this combines deadline urgency, career value and how concrete the current rules are.
Priority 01 · apply now

Danish International Cello Competition

Exceptionally strong career package, free accommodation, substantial cash prizes and a straightforward preselection programme. Deadline 25 Oct 2026.

Priority 02 · concrete 2027 target

David Geringas Cello Competition

Serious three-round professional category, €10,000 first prize, orchestra final and a 10–15 minute free-choice pre-audition video.

Priority 03 · repertoire-efficient

Split International Cello Competition

Clear repertoire, manageable January calendar and repertoire that overlaps strongly with standard professional cello preparation.
